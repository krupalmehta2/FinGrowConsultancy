Frequently Asked Questions

Incubation support, explained clearly

What does incubation support include?

We help founders understand the opportunity, assess eligibility, prepare supporting information, organise documents and stay on track during submission and follow-up.

Do I need an established business to apply?

Requirements differ by programme and founder stage. We review your idea, entity status and documents first, then explain the most suitable route.

Will FinGrow guarantee approval?

No. Approval is decided by the relevant authority or incubator. Our role is to improve clarity, readiness and process discipline.
